Who we are

Process

From brief to live.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Tell us the goal. One service, or a full package, depending on what the brand needs.

  2. 02

    Proposal

    You get the quote, the deliverables, and the timeline before anything starts.

  3. 03

    Onboarding

    Assets, access, and a shared channel. The team and the dates are set at the start.

  4. 04

    Strategy

    We look at the audience, what is not working, and the plan: calendar, scripts, or shoot direction.

  5. 05

    Production

    Videos, graphics, branding, or campaign assets. This is where the plan becomes files.

  6. 06

    Review

    You see the work and ask for changes inside the rounds included. Nothing goes out until you are satisfied.

  7. 07

    Launch

    We deliver the files, or we post and run the rollout, depending on the service.

  8. 08

    Optimize

    On ongoing work, we read what happened and make the next round sharper.

The desk

Roles, not a crowd.

A full stack, used in the combination the brief needs. Not every engagement uses every seat.

Creative director

Owns the direction: the offer, the campaign idea, and how it should sell.

Strategist

Builds the monthly system: angles, hooks, and a calendar that is not random.

Social manager

Posts, replies, watches what people keep, and keeps the page alive.

Paid ads

Runs Meta, TikTok, and Google against leads and sales, then scales what works.

Designer

Campaign frames, ad cuts, carousels, and graphics that match the brand.

Producer

Shoot days, the shot list, and footage that can actually be posted.

Copywriter

Captions, scripts, ads, and the lines a page needs when someone clicks.

Operations

Deadlines, approvals, and the client channel, so the work keeps moving.
